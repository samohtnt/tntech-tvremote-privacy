# TNTech TV Remote — Privacy Policy

_Effective November 17, 2026 (updated to cover the optional purchase)_

TNTech TV Remote ("the app") turns your Android phone into a remote control for Google TV and
Android TV devices. This policy explains what the app does with information. The short version:
**the app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no servers. Nothing you do in it is sent to
us.**

## What the app sends, and where

Everything the app sends goes **directly from your phone to your own TV over your home network**,
encrypted (TLS). It never passes through, or is copied to, any server run by TNTech.

- **Button presses** (arrows, volume, power, and so on) and **app shortcuts** are sent to your TV.
- **Text you type** in the app's keyboard is sent to your TV, to fill in the text box shown there.
- **Microphone audio**: only while you press and hold the microphone button, audio is streamed to
  your TV for its voice search, the same way a physical voice remote works. The app does not
  record, save or keep any audio, and the microphone is never used at any other time. What the TV
  does with your voice search is covered by the TV maker's and Google's privacy policies.

## What the app stores on your phone

To reconnect to your TV automatically, the app keeps on your phone:

- the list of TVs you connected, with their name, model, network address and a security
  fingerprint used to recognise them;
- your app settings (such as vibration and which app buttons to show);
- a security key the phone uses to identify itself to your TVs.

This information stays on your phone. It is not backed up to the cloud and is not shared with
anyone. You can delete it at any time by removing a TV in the app, clearing the app's storage, or
uninstalling the app.

## The optional purchase

The app is free to use. After 30 days it asks, at most once a day, whether you'd like to buy it
for a one-time price. If you buy it, **the payment is handled entirely by Google Play** under
Google's terms and privacy policy. The app only asks Google Play whether this purchase was made,
so it can stop the reminders; we never see your payment details. The app also remembers on your
phone when it was first used and when it last showed the reminder.

## Finding your TV on the network

To find TVs, the app listens for TVs announcing themselves on your Wi-Fi and briefly checks
devices on your home network for the port a TV uses for remote control. This happens only on your
local network, only while the app is open, and the results are not sent anywhere.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Network access, Wi-Fi state, multicast | To find and talk to TVs on your home network |
| Microphone | Voice search, only while you hold the microphone button |

## What we don't do

We don't collect personal information, don't use analytics or advertising, don't sell or share
data, and don't track you across apps or websites.

## Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13, and it doesn't knowingly collect information from
anyone.
